# Code of conduct

Liminal's community is open to everyone who participates in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and consent, especially around microphone-related reports.
- Give credit and preserve asset provenance.
- Accept a maintainer's decision to end an unproductive interaction.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misgendering, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or
when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately through the repository maintainers' GitHub
profiles. Do not expose sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban a participant. Anyone directly involved in a report will recuse
themselves from deciding it when another maintainer is available.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, community
spaces, and public representation of the project.

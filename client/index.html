Free two-player browser horror

Liminal: the creature hears you

Invite a friend or meet a random partner, find three keys, and cross three procedural liminal spaces. Footsteps, creaky floors, and loud microphone input tell the creature where you are.

How does multiplayer work?

Liminal is designed for two players. Invite one friend with a private link or use Quick Play to meet a random partner. Both players share the same deterministic maze, server-authoritative creature, level, and outcome.

Does the monster hear real voices?

Microphone audio never leaves the device. The browser analyzes loudness locally and sends one clamped value as optional game input. Denying microphone access never blocks play.

What happens in a run?

Search dead ends for three keys, avoid noisy floorboards, unlock a thin wall, and move from The Lobby to The Warehouse and finally The Poolrooms.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free and does it need an installation?

It is free for two players and runs in a modern browser with no installation or account.

How many people can play?

The current game is designed for two players connected by private invite or Quick Play.

Is microphone audio recorded or transmitted?

No. Only a local loudness measurement is sent, and microphone access is optional.
